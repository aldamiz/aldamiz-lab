Learning 1 · afibcases.com

Do AI assistants cite a small patient website at all?

Status Measured, repeating weeklyOpened 2026-11-04Next check Every Wednesday

Thesis

A new patient-run site about atrial fibrillation, with data nobody else has, should already show up when ChatGPT or Claude answer the questions patients ask. If it does not, we need to know who they cite instead, and whether the site is missing from their index or simply not chosen.

Defined process

  • 20 patient questions in English, taken from 1,372 real search queries about atrial fibrillation collected on 27 October 2026. Eleven sit close to a published piece; nine are general questions with high demand.
  • Each question goes to ChatGPT (gpt-5.5 with web search) three times and to Claude once, with web search on.
  • For every answer we record the pages it cites, whether afibcases.com is among them, and whether the site is named.
  • Same questions every Wednesday, so weeks can be compared.

Partial results

  • 4 November 2026: 0 of 80 answers cite afibcases.com.
  • Asked directly about afibcases.com, ChatGPT finds the home page and all six pieces and summarises them correctly. The site is in the index; it is just not chosen.
  • On Apple Watch burden, ECG and own-data questions, answers cite Apple (support.apple.com) and the American Heart Association (heart.org). No one with a patient's own data.
  • On "where can I read real experiences", answers cite forums and small sites: connect.mayoclinic.org, a-fib.com, offbeat.community, reddit.
  • Asked the same question three times, ChatGPT cites a different set of sites each time. Of all the sites it cited in any of the three answers, only about 1 in 5 (21%) appeared in all three.
  • Example: for "Why does my AFib mostly happen at night?" the three answers cited 6 different sites between them, and only heart.org was in all three. For "Can alcohol trigger AFib episodes?" no site appeared in all three answers.
  • Claude cites 7.4 pages per answer, ChatGPT 4.0.

What we learn so far

One measurement says nothing. If ChatGPT cites afibcases once in a given week, it may be chance; a change has to hold across the three runs and over several weeks before it means anything. The questions where small sites already get cited are the most open door.

Weekly measurement

Same 20 questions every Wednesday. "Overlap between runs": of all the sites an assistant cites for a question in any of its three answers, the share that appears in all three. At 21%, only about 1 site in 5 is cited every time; the rest come and go, so a single citation in a single week can be chance.
