Does answering the question in the first sentence get a page cited?

Status Running since 7 November 2026Opened 2026-11-07Next check Read on 25 November and 2 December 2026

Thesis

Assistants lift the first lines of a page. A piece that answers the patient's question in its first sentence, with the number inside, should start being cited for that question. The piece on Apple Watch AFib burden had the answer, but not in its first two lines.

Defined process

  • One change only, on afibcases.com/afib-burden-two-percent: a new first sentence with Apple's definition of AFib burden and the case's own number. Three weeks reported as 2% held 19 episodes on 7 of 21 nights, the longest 4.5 hours.
  • The title already carried "afib burden" and the finding, so it was left alone: changing two things at once would not tell which one worked.
  • Measured on questions p01 and p02 of the weekly set, against the 4 November baseline of zero citations.
  • Control: the other five pieces are not touched. If all of them start being cited at once, the cause is the site gaining weight, not the first sentence.
  • Early signal before any citation: an assistant's crawler fetching the page again after the change.
