How long from publishing to being read, and from being read to being cited?

Status Open, collecting dataOpened 2026-11-07Next check With every new piece

Thesis

Being read by an assistant's crawler is not the same as being cited. If there is a long gap between the two, the first weeks after publishing will show nothing however good a piece is, and that is the time to wait before judging it.

Defined process

  • The counter records each crawler visit with its date: ClaudeBot, GPTBot, ChatGPT opening a link, Perplexity and others.
  • For each new piece: date published, first crawler visit, first citation in the weekly set.

Partial results

  • Anthropic's ClaudeBot visited on 28 October 2026. OpenAI's GPTBot has not visited.
  • The only "ChatGPT opening the link" so far came from our own test on 4 November.
