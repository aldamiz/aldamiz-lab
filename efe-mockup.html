Barkibu · Engagement system

Engagement for everyone

A company-wide view of the engagement system — what it is, where the opportunity sits, and how the basic metrics are evolving.

Intro

This dashboard attempts to expose some basic metrics related to the engagement system, and remind of the goals and process.

The Engagement System is the response to the 2024 question "What should we build now that it provides a competitive advantage 2 years from today?", and in this message I described the process, method and a number of related aspects to product development.

The specifics of the system were described here.

Conceptually & methodologically, nothing has changed. The objective remains unchanged: convert clients into referrers.

We have shipped some basic infrastructure: social browsing (streams), interactions (follows, reactions to content), communications (in-app & push notifications), and uses of cashback (donate & deduct).

Areas that come next:

  • Expand the communication layer (new-clients funnel via customer.io, feature discovery, donations trust, linking deductions/donations/claims to the social aspect).
  • Work on the discoverability of social browsing, interactions and uses of cashback.
  • Build a solid narrative — communicate value created by donations, make new clients perceive Barkibu as a place to be with friends, strengthen identity.
The opportunity

A large number of clients come to the app — 68k in April 2026. Many come once a week, and not only for claims-related reasons (link).

From friend-brings-friend (in or out of the referrals program), our performance couldn't be poorer: between 800 and 1,000 referrers per month out of almost 70k monthly uniques. We're perceived as Mapfre, la Mutua, etc. — can we be perceived how we perceive ourselves?

The Engagement System should impact both issues. It seeks something very, very difficult. But we are not at zero anymore.
